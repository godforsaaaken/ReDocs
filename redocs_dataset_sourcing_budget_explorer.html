Fitting Four Reconstruction Pillars Inside a 1 to 3 GB Footprint

Because the offline ReDocs pipeline runs on 4–8 GB RAM CPU-only Windows workstations using pre-trained lightweight OCR and reference-guided template injection rather than training deep vision networks from scratch, multi-gigabyte raw training dumps are unnecessary. This section summarizes the target storage envelope, contrasts full academic layout archives against compact evaluation slices, and highlights the critical Indonesian certificate dataset gap identified in the report.

Critical Finding & Workaround

Bridging the Indonesian Certificate & Statutory Gap

Public ML benchmarks lack scanned Indonesian certificates (Sertifikat) paired with text transcriptions, pixel masks, and editable templates. Meanwhile, Hugging Face's OCR-Data/dataset is a gated 86.7 GB archive with no dataset card, and Scribd Word templates sit behind paywalls.

1. Transparent Stamp & Logo Isolation (StaVer + SignVerOD) Use Kaggle's StaVer (400 scans at 200 DPI with pixel-level binary masks in ground-truth-pixel/) to evaluate OpenCV HSV/luminance alpha extraction, paired with SignVerOD (283.23 MB sample) and tech4humans/signature-detection (147 MB).
2. Statutory Cirebon & BPK Grounding Anchor layout geometry in Permendagri No. 1 Tahun 2023, Permendagri No. 83 Tahun 2022, Peraturan ANRI No. 5 Tahun 2021, public PDFs from JDIH DPRD Kota Cirebon, and the 5.46 MB .docx Template Naskah Dinas pack.
3. Synthetic Round-Trip Certificate Pairs Inject transparent StaVer stamp/logo crops and Indonesian fields into a Jinja2-tagged .docx certificate template, render to PDF, and apply controlled skew/resolution degradation for exact pixel-IoU and CER evaluation.
